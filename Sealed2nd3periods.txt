
This is the Python code for my paper

REPEATED SEALED-BID SECOND-PRICE AUCTION WITH INCONSISTENT 
AND ASYMMETRIC TIME PREFERENCES

-----------------------------------------------------------

ABOUT THE MODEL
Three periods.
Future benefits are discounted by quasi-hyperbolic (beta,delta) discount functions.
Initial budgets, values and time preferecens are randomly drawn.
All players are identical in terms of the distributions of their parameters.
Sophisticated, commitment and naive solutions are calculated.
It is assumed for reasons of tractability that players who lose don't observe the transfer.

-----------------------------------------------------------

MAIN ITERATION LOGIC

Period 3:
1. find b3^*(B3,V3) for every (B3,V3) in grid
2. find distribution of m3 given B3 sample and V3 sample
3. find Eflow3(B3,V3) using b3^*(B3,V3) for every (B3,V3) in grid given distribution of m3

Period 2:
1. find b2^*(B2,V2,V3,delta,beta) for every (B2,V2,V3,delta,beta) in grid
   given Eflow3(B3,V3) by using foc2
2. find distribution of m2 given samples of B2,V2,V3,delta,beta
3. find Eflow2(B2,V2,V3,delta,beta) and Eflow3from2(B2,V2,V3,delta,beta)
   by using b2^*(B2,V2,V3,delta,beta) for every (B2,V2,V3,delta,beta) in grid
   given distribution of m2

Period 1:
1. find b1^*(B1,V1,V2,V3,delta,beta) for every player in sample (not grid)
   given Eflow2 and Eflow3from2 by using foc1

Forward simulation:
1. find new B2 sample and B2 for rivals in each history given b1^*
2. find b2^*(B2,V2,V3,beta,delta) given the draws of (V2,V3,beta,delta) and new B2 sample
3. find new B3 sample and B3 for rival in each history given b2^*
5. Check KS distance between old and new rival sample for B2 and B3 in each history
6. Update B2 and B3 rival samples and break if KS distances are sufficiently small

-----------------------------------------------------

NOTES

***keep the same draw of (B1,V1,V2,V3,delta,beta} during the whole process,
only B2 rivals and B3 rivals are updated in each loop

***In foc2, utility from period 3 is discounted by beta*delta if the solution is sophisticated
or naive and by delta if the solution is commitment. In foc1, future utility from period 2
is always discounted by beta*delta and utility from period 3 is always discounted by
beta*delta^2. In the naive solution, the CV for foc1 are calculated on the assumption that
players in period will follow the behavior of the commitment agent, when in fact they follow
the behavior of the sophisticated agent

-------------------------------------------------------------------

SHAPE AND NAMING CONVENTIONS

N_A : number of auctions
N_P : number of players (per auction, including the focal player)
N_R : number of rivals = N_P - 1
N_H2: number of histories in period 2 (win, lose) = 2
N_H3: number of histories in period 3 (win-win, win-lose, lose-win, lose-lose) = 4

 Common arrays:
   B*_sample:    shape (N_A, N_P)      budgets for all players per auction
   B*_rival_*:   shape (N_A, N_R)      rival budgets per auction and history
   V*_sample:    shape (N_A, N_P)      values for all players per auction
   m2:           shape (N_A, N_H2)     highest rival bid per auction and t=2 history
   m3:           shape (N_A, N_H3)     highest rival bid per auction and t=3 history
   ties2, ties3: same shape as corresponding m* arrays

B3 rival budgets by (H2, H2 outcome) histories:
   winlose: won t=1, lost t=2
   losewin: lost t=1, won t=2
   loselose: lost both t=1, t=2
